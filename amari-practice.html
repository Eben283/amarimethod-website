A first shift is a beginning

Feeling different is not the finish line.

You may come in because your shoulder hurts when you play tennis, your calves keep you from running, or you are tired of planning around a body part.

One session can make a difference. The Practice gives Garrett and you enough time to see if the change holds. If it doesn’t, there is time to keep working.
