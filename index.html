Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Amari Method

What exactly is the Amari Method?
+
The Amari Method is exercise-based pain relief built on a simple idea: you're in pain because something in your body is working too much and something isn't working enough. If you sit hunched at a desk all day, your body needs to open up the other way. Dr. Garrett assesses how you move, identifies where those imbalances are, and teaches you specific exercises to correct them. In a session, you'll work with simple props — yoga blocks, foam rollers, gymnastic rings, sometimes just your own hands — while he physically guides you into correct form. There are 8 core exercises in the full protocol. Unlike PT routines that feel like homework, these exercises are simple and feel good — which is why people actually stick with them. Follow-up sessions refine your technique and introduce new exercises as your body changes.
How quickly will I see results?
+
Most clients experience significant pain relief or complete elimination during their first session as you learn the exercises. The real transformation happens as you master the 8 core exercises and understand your compensation patterns. You'll build a personalized daily practice with exercises that work for your body, maintaining relief and preventing pain from returning.
Is this suitable for my type of pain?
+
The Amari Method works with most types of pain—chronic back pain, neck and shoulder tension, sports injuries, joint pain, and more. However, severe conditions or those requiring medical intervention should be discussed with Dr. Garrett during a free consultation. We'll assess whether the method is right for you and create a personalized plan.
Do I need to be flexible or in shape?
+
Not at all! The exercises are guided by listening to your body and adapted to you exactly as you are right now. Whether you're stiff, out of shape, or recovering from injury, you'll learn modified versions that work for you. The philosophy is "gravitate toward what calls to you"—you'll naturally be drawn to the exercises your body needs most, and we'll meet you exactly where you are.
What's the difference between in-person and virtual sessions?
+
In-person sessions include hands-on teaching of the 8 core exercises and physical guidance through hanging, bridges, and decompression techniques. Virtual sessions work great for learning the exercises through detailed demonstrations and real-time feedback. Many clients start with one in-person session to learn the fundamentals with hands-on support, then continue virtually—both formats teach you the complete method effectively.
How long is each session?
+
Your initial session is 60 minutes and includes a full history, examination, findings discussion, and your first protocol. Follow-up sessions are 50 minutes and focus on checking your progress, refining your protocol, and advancing to the next stage.
Do you accept HSA or FSA payments?
+
Yes — we accept HSA (Health Savings Account) and FSA (Flexible Spending Account) cards for all sessions. Many clients use their HSA/FSA funds to cover the cost of care, making it an even more accessible option for those with qualifying health accounts.
What credentials and training does Dr. Garrett have?
+
Dr. Garrett is a Doctor of Chiropractic from Life Chiropractic College West in Hayward, CA with 25+ years of hands-on experience as a chiropractor, massage therapist, and yoga teacher. He developed the Amari Method after discovering that teaching clients specific exercises produced longer-lasting results than adjustments alone. He's helped 200+ clients achieve lasting pain relief.
What if the method doesn't work for me?
+
If you don't experience noticeable relief after your first session, we keep working with you until you do — at no additional charge. We don't stop until you feel the difference.
Can I do the online program if I've never tried the method?
+
Yes! The Living Practice program includes 53 videos that teach all 8 core exercises plus bonus techniques in detail. It's designed to guide you step-by-step through the complete method. However, many clients find that starting with at least one session with Dr. Garrett helps them understand their specific compensation patterns and get personalized guidance on which exercises to prioritize, which enhances the self-paced program experience.
Do you accept insurance?
+
We do not bill insurance directly. However, we can provide you with a detailed superbill (itemized receipt) that you can submit to your insurance company for potential reimbursement. Many clients have successfully received partial or full reimbursement for alternative care services. We recommend checking with your insurance provider about their coverage for out-of-network wellness services before your first session.
