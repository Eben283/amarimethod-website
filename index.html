Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from a chiropractor?
Chiropractors typically manipulate joints to realign your spine. While this can provide temporary relief, the pain often returns. The Amari Method teaches your nervous system to restore alignment naturally through specific movements and awareness. You learn tools you can use for life.
How quickly will I feel relief?
Most clients experience noticeable relief in their first session. You'll learn and practice specific tools that immediately reduce pain and tension. Relief deepens as you continue applying these tools at home.
Will I need ongoing treatment?
Unlike traditional therapies, the Amari Method teaches you to heal yourself. Many clients don't need ongoing appointments after learning the tools—they use them independently. Some choose follow-up sessions for continued refinement, but you're never dependent.
Can I do this virtually?
Yes. Dr. Garrett offers both in-person sessions in San Francisco and full virtual sessions nationwide. The virtual sessions are just as effective—you'll receive the same assessment, guidance, and tools.
What if I'm skeptical?
Skepticism is healthy. Our satisfaction guarantee means you have nothing to lose. If you don't experience relief, we keep working until you do. Start with a consultation to discuss your specific situation—no pressure.
