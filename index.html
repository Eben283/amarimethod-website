Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Amari Method

What exactly is the Amari Method?
▼
The Amari Method is a revolutionary pain relief approach that combines deep anatomical knowledge with intuitive body awareness. Rather than treating symptoms, it identifies and addresses the root causes of pain. Dr. Garrett developed this method over 25+ years of practice, helping thousands of clients achieve lasting relief without drugs or surgery.
How quickly will I see results?
▼
Most clients experience significant pain relief or complete elimination during their first session. The real transformation happens as you learn the tools and techniques—typically 2-4 sessions are enough for lasting results. You'll also receive daily practices to maintain your relief and prevent pain from returning.
Is this suitable for my type of pain?
▼
The Amari Method works with most types of pain—chronic back pain, neck and shoulder tension, sports injuries, joint pain, and more. However, severe conditions or those requiring medical intervention should be discussed with Dr. Garrett during a free consultation. We'll assess whether the method is right for you and create a personalized plan.
Do I need to be flexible or in shape?
▼
Not at all! The method is designed to work with your body exactly as it is right now. Whether you're stiff, out of shape, or recovering from injury, the techniques are adapted to your needs. Dr. Garrett's approach meets you where you are and gently guides you toward greater mobility and pain relief.
What's the difference between in-person and virtual sessions?
▼
In-person sessions include hands-on alignment adjustments, which many clients find valuable for immediate relief. Virtual sessions are perfect if you're unable to visit in person—you'll receive expert guidance, demonstrations, and can ask questions in real-time. Most clients start with one in-person session to learn the fundamentals, then continue virtually if preferred.
How long is each session?
▼
Sessions typically last 60-90 minutes, depending on your specific needs and the assessment Dr. Garrett conducts. The first session is longer as it includes a thorough evaluation, education about your condition, and hands-on experience with the method. Follow-up sessions are typically 60 minutes.
What credentials and training does Dr. Garrett have?
▼
Dr. Garrett brings 25+ years of hands-on experience helping thousands of clients achieve lasting pain relief. His approach combines rigorous anatomical understanding with intuitive body awareness gained through decades of practice. He regularly continues his education to stay current with the latest body science and healing techniques.
What if the method doesn't work for me?
▼
We're confident in the method's effectiveness, but we understand that everyone's body is unique. If you're not satisfied after your first session, we'll discuss your experience and determine the best path forward—whether that's adjusting the approach or exploring other options. Your satisfaction is our priority.
Can I do the online program if I've never tried the method?
▼
Yes! The online program includes comprehensive instructional videos that guide you through the complete method. However, many clients find that starting with at least one in-person or virtual session with Dr. Garrett helps them understand their specific pain patterns and get personalized guidance, which enhances the online program experience.
