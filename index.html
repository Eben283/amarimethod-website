Questions

The things people ask before they book.

What exactly is the Amari Method?
You're in pain because your body is out of balance — something is working too hard because something else isn't working enough. Dr. Garrett assesses how your body moves, identifies where the imbalance is, and guides you through rebalancing protocols using simple props like yoga blocks, foam rollers, and gymnastic rings. You're not lying on a table while someone works on you. You're moving, hanging, finding positions where your body starts to rebalance itself. There are 8 core protocols, and you learn them at your own pace. They feel good while you do them, which is why people actually stick with them — and you take them home.
How quickly will I see results?
Most clients feel significant relief during their first session. The deeper transformation happens over time as your body learns to hold its own balance. You'll build a daily practice, about 5 minutes a day, that maintains what you gained and keeps going deeper.
Is this suitable for my type of pain?
The Amari Method works with most types of pain — chronic back pain, neck and shoulder tension, sports injuries, joint pain, and more. Severe conditions or those requiring medical intervention should be discussed with Dr. Garrett during a free consultation.
How long is each session?
Your initial session is 60 minutes. Follow-up sessions are 50 minutes. Both are available in person (San Francisco) or virtually.
What if the method doesn't work for me?
If you don't experience noticeable relief after your first session, we keep working with you until you do, at no additional charge.
Do you accept HSA, FSA, or insurance?
We accept HSA and FSA cards for all sessions. We don't bill insurance directly, but we can provide a superbill you can submit for potential reimbursement.
