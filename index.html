Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Amari Method

What exactly is the Amari Method?
▼
The Amari Method isn't a treatment—it's a teaching. Dr. Garrett guides you to understand what your body is doing and why. Through 7 core exercises, you'll learn to identify your compensation patterns and give your body what it's been asking for. It's permission-based movement: you learn to work with your body's wisdom instead of forcing it into submission. Developed from 25+ years of experience, over 200 clients have already found lasting relief.
How quickly will I see results?
▼
Most clients experience significant pain relief or complete elimination during their first session as you learn the exercises. The real transformation happens as you master the 7 core exercises and understand your compensation patterns—typically 2-4 sessions are enough for lasting results. You'll build a personalized daily practice with exercises that work for your body, maintaining relief and preventing pain from returning.
Is this suitable for my type of pain?
▼
The Amari Method works with most types of pain—chronic back pain, neck and shoulder tension, sports injuries, joint pain, and more. However, severe conditions or those requiring medical intervention should be discussed with Dr. Garrett during a free consultation. We'll assess whether the method is right for you and create a personalized plan.
Do I need to be flexible or in shape?
▼
Not at all! The exercises are permission-based and adapted to your body exactly as it is right now. Whether you're stiff, out of shape, or recovering from injury, you'll learn modified versions that work for you. The philosophy is "gravitate toward what calls to you"—you'll naturally be drawn to the exercises your body needs most, and we'll meet you exactly where you are.
What's the difference between in-person and virtual sessions?
▼
In-person sessions include hands-on teaching of the 7 core exercises and physical guidance through hanging, bridges, and decompression techniques. Virtual sessions work great for learning the exercises through detailed demonstrations and real-time feedback. Many clients start with one in-person session to learn the fundamentals with hands-on support, then continue virtually—both formats teach you the complete method effectively.
How long is each session?
▼
Sessions typically last 60-90 minutes, depending on your specific needs and the assessment Dr. Garrett conducts. The first session is longer as it includes a thorough evaluation, education about your condition, and hands-on experience with the method. Follow-up sessions are typically 60 minutes.
What credentials and training does Dr. Garrett have?
▼
Dr. Garrett brings 25+ years of hands-on experience helping thousands of clients achieve lasting pain relief. His approach combines rigorous anatomical understanding with intuitive body awareness gained through decades of practice. He regularly continues his education to stay current with the latest body science and healing techniques.
What if the method doesn't work for me?
▼
We're confident in the method's effectiveness, but we understand that everyone's body is unique. If you're not satisfied after your first session, we'll discuss your experience and determine the best path forward—whether that's adjusting the approach or exploring other options. Your satisfaction is our priority.
Can I do the online program if I've never tried the method?
▼
Yes! The Living Practice program includes 53 videos that teach all 7 core exercises plus bonus techniques in detail. It's designed to guide you step-by-step through the complete method. However, many clients find that starting with at least one session with Dr. Garrett helps them understand their specific compensation patterns and get personalized guidance on which exercises to prioritize, which enhances the self-paced program experience.
