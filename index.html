Questions

What people ask before their first visit.

Straight answers, no sales pitch.

Does the Amari Method take insurance, or can I use my HSA or FSA?+
The Amari Method does not bill health insurance directly. You pay for each session yourself. Many HSA and FSA plans allow reimbursement for care like this, but it depends on your specific plan, so check with your plan administrator before booking.
How is the Amari Method different from physical therapy?+
Physical therapy usually focuses on one specific injury, with exercises prescribed over a set course of visits, often after surgery or a referral. The Amari Method looks at your whole body to find why the pain is there, then teaches you protocols you keep for good. It is not a replacement for medically necessary PT. Many clients come to Garrett after physical therapy hasn't given them lasting relief.
Does the Amari Method help with sciatica, a herniated disc, or TMJ and jaw clenching?+
Yes, these are common reasons people come in. Clients dealing with sciatica, disc-related pain, or jaw clenching have found relief by rebalancing the areas working too hard around the painful spot, instead of working on the spot alone. One client expected a herniated disc to mean surgery and avoided it. Another came in for back pain and found her jaw clenching eased too. Severe or urgent conditions should be checked with a medical professional first, and Garrett will say plainly whether the method is a fit.
Where in San Francisco do you see clients?+
Garrett sees clients in person in San Francisco and works with people across the Bay Area, plus virtually anywhere in the world. You get the exact location and directions when you book. Virtual sessions cover the same protocols over video, so distance isn't a barrier.
Is the Amari Method backed by science? Is it legit?+
The Amari Method isn't a drug or a procedure. It's grounded in how the body moves and rebalances. The same principles show up in established movement practices like Feldenkrais and Pilates. What most people trust is the first session: most clients feel a real difference before they leave, and if you don't, Garrett keeps working with you at no extra charge. Garrett built the method over 25+ years of hands-on work.
What is the Living Practice program, and can I buy it on its own?+
Living Practice is a video program that teaches all 8 core protocols plus extra techniques, so you can practice at home at your own pace. It's included with the 8-session series, and you can also buy it on its own for $347. Many clients pair it with at least one session with Garrett so they know where their body is out of balance before they start.
