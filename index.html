How is this different from a chiropractor? +
Chiropractors typically manipulate joints to realign your spine. While this can provide temporary relief, many patients find the pain returns. The Amari Method teaches your nervous system to restore alignment naturally through specific movements and awareness. You learn tools you can use for life, not just during appointments.

Most clients experience noticeable relief in their first session. You'll learn and practice specific tools that immediately reduce pain and tension. Relief deepens as you continue applying these tools at home.

Unlike traditional therapies, the Amari Method teaches you to heal yourself. Many clients don't need ongoing appointments after learning the tools—they use them independently. Some choose follow-up sessions for continued refinement, but you're never dependent.
