Study consent + publishing opt-in
Templated for all studies. [Condition] and [questionnaire] fill from the registry.
Shown as a checkbox at signup, reconfirmed on paper at session 1.

--- CONSENT (checkbox at signup) ---

I'm joining the Amari Method [Condition] case series. Here's what that means:

- Three free 15-minute in-person sessions at 662 8th Ave, San Francisco.
- These are Amari Method rebalancing sessions, not medical care. Garrett teaches
  and guides the movement work.
- At each session I'll rate my pain 0 to 10 before and after. At the first and
  last session I'll fill out a short questionnaire about how [Condition] affects
  me.
- Taking part is my choice. I can stop after any session with no obligation.
- After the study wraps, Amari Method may publish the combined results as a case
  series. If my own words or scores are used, only my first name appears, and
  only if I opt in below.

[ ] I agree to take part on these terms.
[ ] You can use my first name and results in the published case series. (optional)

--- WHAT TO EXPECT (plain-language expander) ---

The study documents what participants report after three short sessions. We
track two things: a quick 0 to 10 pain rating each visit, and a recognized
questionnaire at the start and end so the change is measured the same way for
everyone. We publish the group results, including the limits of a small study
like this one. Garrett teaches and guides the sessions. Nothing here
replaces care from your own clinician.
