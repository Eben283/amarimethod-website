The Jaw Tension Study

A free 3-session study for SF adults with long-term jaw tension. We track how your jaw feels before and after each session.

Study details

Why it works

Jaw tension like this comes from some of the muscles that move the jaw working too hard while others aren’t working enough. The protocol rebalances them, which settles the tension.

Who’s running it

Amari Method, a somatic movement practice in San Francisco.

What to expect

Three 15-minute sessions at our office, 662 8th Ave. No cost. No obligation past session 1 if it’s not helping.

What happens with the results

When the study closes at 30 participants, we’ll publish the full findings, the before-and-after scores across the whole cohort. Participants are identified by first name only, and only with their consent.
