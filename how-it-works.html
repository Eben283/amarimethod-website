How Body Alignment Fixes Back Pain (When Nothing Else Works)

Understanding the Amari Method's 7-step protocol for lasting relief

The Problem with Traditional Approaches

Why Chiropractors Aren't Enough

Chiropractors manipulate your spine to realign vertebrae. This provides temporary relief—but your body returns to misalignment because the underlying cause (nervous system dysfunction) wasn't addressed.

Result: Ongoing appointments. Permanent dependency. The pain always comes back.

Why Physical Therapy Falls Short

Physical therapists prescribe exercises to strengthen muscles. While strength helps, it doesn't teach your nervous system to maintain alignment naturally. You're left doing exercises forever.

Result: Temporary relief. Heavy exercise burden. Slow progress.

Why Painkillers Aren't the Answer

Pain medication masks the problem. Your body stays misaligned, the underlying cause gets worse, and you become dependent on medication.

Result: No healing. Dependency. More serious problems develop.

The Amari Method Solution: Body Alignment Through Nervous System Restoration

Step 1: Assessment (Awakening)

We perform a comprehensive assessment to identify the exact patterns beneath your symptoms. We measure your alignment, assess your movement patterns, and understand how your nervous system is holding tension.

What you learn: Why your body is misaligned and what's maintaining that misalignment.

Step 2: Release (Connection)

Using specific, gentle movements and breathing techniques, we help your nervous system release the tension it's been holding. This is where immediate relief happens—you feel the difference in your first session.

What you learn: Which movements and breathing patterns create instant relief for YOUR body.

Step 3: Alignment Restoration (Freedom)

Your body naturally returns to alignment as you continue applying the tools you've learned. Unlike static exercises, these are simple practices that integrate into daily life—you literally move differently without thinking about it.

What you learn: How to maintain alignment through natural movement and awareness.

Step 4: Prevention (Mastery)

You develop the awareness and skills to recognize early signs of misalignment and correct them immediately—before pain develops. Prevention becomes automatic.

What you learn: How to catch and correct misalignment before it becomes pain.

Step 5: Life Transformation

With proper alignment restored and maintained, you rediscover activities you thought were gone forever. Sleeping through the night. Playing with grandkids. Sports. Work without pain.

What you achieve: Life without pain, without dependency, without ongoing appointments.

The Key Difference

The Amari Method teaches your nervous system to maintain alignment, not just your muscles. Once you learn it, you've got it for life.
