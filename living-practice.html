What it is

The same method Garrett teaches in the room, on video.

Living Practice teaches the same Amari Method used in one-on-one work, adapted for home use. Each lesson gives you context, clear technical language, guided instruction, and the common mistakes to avoid, so you can run the work on your own without guessing what comes next.

It is a standalone program, built for people in pain who want a calm, repeatable daily practice. For many people, it fully replaces the need to work in person.

What's inside.

53 guided videos arranged in a linear progression, mirroring how the method is taught in person.

Guided lessons

One lesson at a time, in the order the method is taught.

Most videos are under 4 minutes, with just over 2½ hours of instruction in total, so the practice stays within reach even on low-energy days. A small number of longer sessions are included where deeper explanation is essential.
