What it is

You already do this work in the room. Now it comes home.

In a session, Garrett sets up the props and guides you through the protocols while he watches how your body responds. Living Practice takes those same protocols and puts them on video, so you can run them on your own without guessing what comes next.

It is a standalone program. You do not need to be mid-series to use it, and it keeps working long after your last visit.

What's inside.

Eight follow-along videos, one for each core protocol, plus a short home practice you can run in about five minutes.

Follow-along videos

One protocol at a time, guided end to end.

Each protocol gets its own video. Garrett walks you through the setup, shows you the movement, and coaches the pace, the same way he would in the room. You press play and move with him.

  • A short daily practice that strings them together for about five minutes
