The Amari Method: A Gentle Path to Back Pain Relief

7 min read • February 2026 • By Dr. Garrett Hewstan

Over 25 years and 5,000+ clients, the Amari Method has achieved a 90% success rate in providing lasting back pain relief. Here's why it works when other approaches fail.

The End of Your Busy Day

It's the end of a busy day, and your low back is screaming again. The familiar routine begins. You take some Advil and pour a glass of wine. You think about starting yoga again, but who has the energy for an hour-long class? Maybe you should book a massage. The cycle feels endless with no lasting results.

But what if there was another way?

A Different Approach to Back Pain

The Amari Method teaches you to take your body back. The approach is beautifully simple—using gentle movements and occasional props like a yoga block, foam roller, or gymnastic rings. Each step requires minimal effort but yields profound results. Most importantly, the Amari Method treats your entire body as an interconnected system. Back pain doesn't occur in isolation—it's a symptom of misalignment throughout your body that has cascaded from head to toe.

When clients first experience the Amari Method, they're usually surprised at how much relief they get within minutes. One recent client described it as "swimming underwater." Another shared, "I feel weightless." Most experience themselves as feeling 'much lighter.' Many report sensing energy and blood flowing to parts of their body they hadn't felt in years. One client even described it as "reconnecting to the journey in the womb."

These aren't just exercises—they're protocols for reconnecting with your body in ways you've probably never experienced before.

"It's like swimming underwater. I feel weightless. I feel lighter. I can feel energy flowing to parts of my body I haven't felt in years."

Work With Your Body, Not Against It

Imagine completing these simple protocols each day and finding yourself deeply rested, even without more sleep. These aren't your typical exercises—they work you instead of you working them.

Unlike traditional exercise where an instructor tells you what to do, The Amari Method empowers you to tune into what feels right to you in the moment. Your body leads the way—there's no planning the next move because your body intuitively knows where to go. The experience is intimate, almost beyond description. For many, it's the first time they've been encouraged to move entirely at their own pace.

This is the key difference. You're not forcing your body into positions. You're not following a rigid plan. Instead, you're developing a conversation with your body—listening to it, respecting it, moving with it rather than pushing against it.

Creating Your Sacred Space

Our journey begins with creating a sacred space in your home—your private sanctuary for these protocols. Start with what feels most natural to you. One of the most beautiful signs of progress is how the exercises become increasingly effortless. You're not just doing movements—you're developing a profound self-connection.

This isn't about dedication or discipline in the traditional sense. It's about discovering that taking care of your body feels good. That moving gently and mindfully is actually more effective than pushing hard. That rest and recovery are not luxuries—they're necessities that your body has been asking for all along.

Restoring Natural Balance

Modern life has pulled us far from our natural state of balance. We spend our days hunched over screens, our back muscles overworking while our front body grows inactive. We swing between frantic activity and complete exhaustion, losing touch with our body's innate wisdom.

The Amari Method helps restore this lost equilibrium, guiding you back to a state of natural harmony—both physically and mentally. It's time to rediscover what your body already knows.

When you're balanced and aligned:

  • Pain diminishes naturally as dysfunction is addressed
  • You experience improved energy and vitality
  • Your body feels lighter and more fluid
  • You develop a deeper connection with yourself
  • The relief is lasting because you've fixed the root cause

No More Endless Cycles

You don't need to stay stuck in the pain-pill-massage cycle. The Amari Method offers a genuine alternative—not a quick fix, but a real solution.

By developing a daily practice with these protocols, you're taking control of your own healing. You're no longer dependent on appointments, medications, or hope that something external will fix you. You become the agent of your own transformation.

And the best part? It feels good while you're doing it. These aren't grueling workouts. They're gentle, intuitive, and deeply rewarding.

FAQ: The Amari Method & Back Pain Relief

What makes the Amari Method different from regular exercise?

The Amari Method isn't about pushing your body to work harder. Instead, you work with your body's innate wisdom. The protocols work you instead of you working them. You move at your own pace, tuning into what feels right in the moment.

Do I need special equipment?

No. The Amari Method uses minimal props—a yoga block, foam roller, or gymnastic rings are helpful, but not required. Most of the work happens with your body and gravity.

How quickly will I feel relief?

Many clients experience significant relief within minutes of starting the protocols. The effects deepen with consistent practice. Most report feeling lighter, more energized, and more connected to their body immediately.

Can I do the Amari Method at home?

Yes. The beauty of the Amari Method is that it can be done anywhere. Many clients create a sacred space in their home—a quiet area where they can practice these protocols daily. Most people practice 10-15 minutes daily, which is essential for maintaining alignment and preventing pain recurrence.

Is the Amari Method just for back pain?

While we focus on back pain here, the Amari Method addresses misalignment throughout your entire body. Clients report relief from neck pain, shoulder tension, hip discomfort, and more.

What does 'your body leads the way' mean?

Unlike traditional exercise where an instructor tells you what to do, the Amari Method trusts your body's innate intelligence. You move intuitively, following what feels right. Your body knows where it needs to go.

Why do people describe it as 'swimming underwater'?

The protocols create a weightless feeling, as if you're suspended in water. Many clients report feeling deeply rested and reconnected to their body in ways they've never experienced before. Some even describe it as reconnecting to their natural state.

Will I still need painkillers?

Many clients find they no longer need pain medication once they establish the Amari Method protocols. By addressing the root cause of pain rather than just managing symptoms, lasting relief is possible.
