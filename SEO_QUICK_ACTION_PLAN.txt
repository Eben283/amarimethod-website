🔍 TASK 2: ADD SCHEMA MARKUP (15 min)

Why: Schema markup tells Google exactly what your content means.
Result: Shows "rich snippets" in search results (reviews, ratings, etc.)

What to do:

Copy this code and add it to the <head> section of index.html
(after the title tag, before the closing </head>)

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

<script type="application/ld+json">
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "LocalBusiness",
  "name": "Amari Method",
  "description": "Body alignment therapy for pain relief in San Francisco",
  "url": "https://www.amarimethod.com",
  "telephone": "[YOUR-PHONE-NUMBER]",
  "address": {
    "@type": "PostalAddress",
    "streetAddress": "[YOUR-ADDRESS]",
    "addressLocality": "San Francisco",
    "addressRegion": "CA",
    "postalCode": "94102",
    "addressCountry": "US"
  },
  "areaServed": "San Francisco, CA",
  "serviceType": "Pain Relief Therapy",
  "priceRange": "$$"
}
</script>

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Replace [YOUR-PHONE-NUMBER] and [YOUR-ADDRESS] with your actual info.

🔄 ALSO ADD TO: booking.html, about.html
