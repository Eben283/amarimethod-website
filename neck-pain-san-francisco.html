Your Neck Is the Victim — Not the Cause

Most neck pain treatments target the wrong place.

Cervical Manipulation

Adjusting the vertebrae restores mobility — but for that mobility to hold, the muscle imbalances and movement patterns that pulled those joints out of place also need to change.

Result: Lasting relief requires addressing both the joints and the patterns.

Massage Therapy

Releasing tight traps and levator scapulae muscles feels good — but tightness returns because those muscles are overworking to compensate for a postural problem that wasn't addressed.

Result: Temporary relief. Tension rebuilds within days.

Generic Neck Exercises

Chin tucks and cervical stretches treat the neck in isolation. If your thoracic spine is stiff or your shoulder blades are mispositioned, those exercises can't hold.

Result: Slow progress. Pain persists or gets worse.

The Real Cause of Most Chronic Neck Pain

It starts below the neck — and it's highly correctable.

Forward Head Posture

The average head weighs 10–12 pounds. For every inch it sits forward of the shoulders, your neck muscles carry an extra 10 pounds of load. Most people's heads are 2–3 inches forward — that's 20–30 extra pounds on the neck all day.

Thoracic Spine Stiffness

When your mid-back rounds and stiffens, your body compensates by extending the neck — pulling the head forward and compressing the cervical joints. Releasing the thoracic spine is often the fastest path to lasting neck relief.

Shoulder Blade Mechanics

The muscles that position your shoulder blades attach directly to your neck and upper back. When the scapulae collapse forward, the neck muscles compensate by overworking to hold your head up. Fixing scapular mechanics reduces neck load dramatically.

Frequently Asked Questions — Neck Pain

What actually causes chronic neck pain?
▼
Chronic neck pain is almost never caused by the neck itself. It's usually driven by forward head posture — where the head sits too far in front of the shoulders. For every inch the head is forward, it adds approximately 10 pounds of load on the neck muscles. The Amari Method identifies the postural pattern driving this and corrects it at the source.
Why does my neck keep going out even after chiropractic adjustments?
▼
Adjustments restore joint mobility, which is valuable. The Amari Method builds on that by also correcting the muscle imbalances and movement patterns that pulled those joints out of position — so your alignment holds long-term.
Can neck pain cause headaches and arm numbness?
▼
Yes. Neck misalignment commonly causes tension headaches, occipital headaches, and symptoms that radiate into the shoulders, arms, and hands. When compressed nerves and restricted blood flow are the cause, correcting the cervical alignment relieves these secondary symptoms as well.
