Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 14, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Amari Method ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our services.

Information We Collect

  • Contact information (such as name, email address, phone number)
  • Communication preferences
  • Any other information you choose to provide

Information Automatically Collected

We use Google Analytics to collect standard internet log information and details of visitor behavior patterns. This information is only processed in a way that does not directly identify anyone. We use this information to:

  • Analyze how our services are used
  • Improve our services
  • Monitor website performance

Meta Pixel and Conversions API

We use Meta's advertising tools, including the Meta Pixel and Conversions API, to better understand how users engage with our website and to serve relevant advertisements across Meta platforms (such as Facebook and Instagram). These tools collect information such as your device, browser, and activity on our site. This information may be shared with Meta and used in accordance with Meta's Data Policy. You can manage your ad preferences and opt-out of personalized advertising through your Facebook Ads Settings.

Text Message Communications

When you opt-in to receive text messages from us, we collect:

  • Your mobile phone number
  • Your text message consent preferences
  • Date and time of consent
  • Message interaction history

Important Notice Regarding Text Message Data: All text messaging originator opt-in data and consent information will not be shared with any third parties, excluding aggregators and providers of the Text Message services.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and improve our services
  • Respond to your requests and inquiries
  • Send you important service updates
  • Deliver text message communications you have opted into
  • Analyze and improve our website performance through Google Analytics
  • Comply with legal obligations

Security

We maintain appropriate administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These security measures include:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Regular security assessments and monitoring
  • Access controls and authentication requirements
  • Secure data storage systems
  • Regular security updates and patches
  • Employee training on data security practices

However, please note that no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience. Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit our website. We use:

Essential Cookies

  • Required for basic website functionality
  • Cannot be disabled as they are necessary for the website to work properly

Analytics Cookies

  • Used by Google Analytics to collect anonymous information about how visitors use our website
  • Help us understand patterns of user behavior and improve our website

Preference Cookies

  • Remember your choices and preferences on our website
  • Improve your browsing experience

You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings. Most web browsers allow you to:

  • View your cookie settings
  • Accept or reject cookies
  • Delete existing cookies

Please note that disabling certain cookies may impact the functionality of our website.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Opt-out of text messages by replying STOP
  • Control cookies through your browser settings

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our website. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect standard internet log information and visitor behavior information in an anonymous form. The information generated by Google Analytics cookies about your use of the website is transmitted to Google. This information is processed to compile statistical reports on website activity for us. We have activated the IP anonymization feature in Google Analytics to ensure an additional layer of privacy protection.

External Links

Our website may contain links to other websites. This Privacy Policy applies only to our website. When you click on links to other websites, you will leave our website and this Policy will not apply to your use of and activity on those other websites. We recommend you consult the privacy policies of those other websites to understand how they collect, use, and process your information. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of such other websites. Visiting these other websites is at your own risk.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
