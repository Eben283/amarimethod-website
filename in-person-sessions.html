Benefits of In-Person Sessions

In-Person Work

Garrett sees your movement in real time and cues you in the moment — that immediate feedback is hard to replicate over video.

Personalized Attention

One-on-one focus in a private, dedicated space. Garrett works exclusively with you during your session.

Complete Assessment

In-person evaluation allows for thorough movement assessment and real-time cues based on your body's response.

Immediate Results

Feel the difference during your session. Many clients experience significant pain relief within the first hour.

Private Space

Your session takes place in a private, dedicated space. No shared waiting rooms, no distractions. Just you and Garrett, focused on your pain.

In-Depth Learning

Learn techniques and practices with direct guidance. Understanding your body's mechanics leads to lasting results.
