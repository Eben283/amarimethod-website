The session

Your in-person session, start to finish.

Sixty minutes total, one on one, in the studio.

01 Before you arrive

Wear something you can move in

Stretchy pants or shorts, a t-shirt or athletic top. Nothing else to bring or prepare. Every prop you'll need is here.

02 About 20 minutes

The assessment, in the room

How you stand, move, and hold yourself is read from every angle. The focus is the pattern behind the pain, what's overworking and what's gone quiet.

03 About 30 minutes

Your protocol, hands-on

Using the rings, a yoga block, or the foam roller, you're guided through the protocol your body is asking for, adjusted as your body responds, moment to moment.

04 Last 10 minutes

What you leave with

Most people notice the shift walking out: taller, lighter, less braced. You also leave with a five-minute take-home practice to hold it until your next session.
