Before you book

Things people ask before they book.

Q.01

What if I book the initial session and don't want to continue?

+
No commitment beyond the $225. After your first session you'll know whether the method is right for you. Most clients want to continue. Some don't, and that's fine. You leave with the take-home practice either way.
Q.02

Why do all the series start with the same $225 session?

+
Because every series begins with the same hour: a full assessment, your first guided protocol, and a take-home practice. There's no point committing to a package before you've spent a session with Dr. Garrett. Pay $225 today. If you continue, your $225 is credited toward whichever series you choose.
Q.03

How do I upgrade to a series after my first session?

+
After your initial session, Dr. Garrett will walk you through what he sees and what makes sense for you. If you decide to continue, your $225 is credited toward the 4-pack or 8-pack and the upgrade is processed through your client portal. No re-booking, no extra step.
Q.04

HSA, FSA, insurance?

+
HSA and FSA are accepted on all sessions and series. We don't bill insurance directly, but we provide a superbill on request that you can submit for potential reimbursement. Affirm is available at checkout for series.
Q.05

What if the method doesn't work for me?

+
If you don't experience noticeable relief after your first session, we keep working with you until you do, at no additional charge. Most clients feel a difference before they leave the room.
