Fresh worklist against the live feat/new-site wiring. Unique page heroes, partner tiles, hip/knee doorway-window winners, and the sharp Annie/Elise/Victor/Tyler testimonials are done — skip them. This pack is only what still blocks brand quality: journal bad-frames, a few wire-fixes, and real-client testimonial re-sources.

How to use a REFINE card (e.g. Passive bridge):
1. The photo shows on the card. Click it to open the full JPG (or use the Photo URL under the card).
2. Save that JPG → drag it into Adobe Firefly.
3. Click Copy → paste the prompt into Firefly → run.
4. “Attached” in the prompt = the photo you dropped in. Generate cards (section 2) have no base photo.

Rules (from 2026-07-11 nano-banana / Firefly rulebook): person is the hero, equipment is context · foam roller = plain smooth black only · yoga block = real Hugger Mugger foam (matte foam texture), never cork/wood swap as the default, never blue plastic · never a massage table · no pain gestures · no cars · composition > backdrop swap (person inside a shallow scene with a caught moment, not squared-up to a flat plane) · ration walls and low bookshelves · elevated-casual wardrobe for 33–48, fabric varied off linen · affluent tidy Marin/Berkeley, shallow background · full body then crop in post · under 1024 chars · refine prompts name only what that exact frame contains.

Taste gate (2026-07-15): handsome / pretty / a little wow is OK. Sultry / lingerie / “almost naked” is not. Fully clothed with real fabric covering chest and midriff. No camisole or open plunging neckline as the wardrobe. No bare chest, no sports-bra crop as the hero, no shirtless men unless the shot is clearly athletic training and still reads as dressed for the gym. Attraction lives in face, carriage, fabric, and light — not skin as the subject.
