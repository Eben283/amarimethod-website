What Causes Back Pain: The Alignment Connection

Your body is an interconnected system. When even one area is misaligned, it creates a chain reaction of compensation and pain.

Common Misalignment Patterns That Cause Back Pain:

  • Forward head posture - Your head shifts forward, forcing your back muscles to compensate, creating tension and pain
  • Twisted pelvis - Pelvic misalignment forces your spine into unnatural positions, causing chronic lower back pain
  • Rounded shoulders - Shoulder misalignment puts stress on the entire spine, causing mid and lower back pain
  • Locked ankles - Surprisingly, ankle misalignment affects hip alignment, which cascades up to the spine
  • Collapsed chest - Chest misalignment restricts breathing and forces the spine to work harder, creating upper back pain

The breakthrough insight: Fix these misalignments, and your back pain disappears. No surgery. No lifelong medication. Just proper alignment.
