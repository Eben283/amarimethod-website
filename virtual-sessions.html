How virtual works

The same assessment, through a camera.

Before your session, get a yoga block and clear a few feet of floor. Prop your phone or laptop so your full body is in frame, and you'll get help adjusting the angle once the call starts.

From there it runs the same as in-person: he watches how you stand and move, finds what's overworking and what's gone quiet, and guides you through the protocol your body is asking for. You leave with the same five-minute take-home practice as anyone in the studio.

FAQ

Questions about working over video.

Does this actually work over video?+
Yes. You do the movement in every session, in person or virtual, and you're guided either way. Most clients get the same shift virtually that they would in the studio.
What do I need for a virtual session?+
A yoga block, a phone or laptop you can prop up, and a few feet of open floor. You'll get help setting the angle once the call starts.
What if my connection drops?+
Reconnect and pick up where you left off. If it happens repeatedly, we'll find another time to finish the session at no extra cost.
Can I switch between virtual and in-person?+
Yes. Some clients stay virtual the whole way through, others alternate depending on the week. Book whichever fits from your client portal.
What if the session doesn't work for me?+
If you don't feel noticeable relief by the end of your first session, we keep working with you at no additional charge until you do.
