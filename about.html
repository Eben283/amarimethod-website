Named for a boy whose story changed everything.

Garrett Hewstan knows firsthand the power of natural healing. As a child, he spent countless nights in hospital oxygen tents, struggling to breathe through severe asthma. Everything changed when his mother took him to a practitioner who helped him get off medication and gave him the freedom to thrive. That experience set him on the path to studying the body professionally.

Years later, during his final year of training, Garrett had an experience that would shape his life's mission. A woman named Phyllis shared heartbreaking news: her 11-year-old grandson, Amari, had just passed away from complications of asthma.

The story struck Garrett deeply. Amari was the same age he had been when natural care saved his life. Garrett couldn't help but wonder: What if I could have helped him?

· · ·
The Promise
In that moment, Garrett made a promise, to Amari, to his younger self, and to every person struggling with chronic pain, that he would dedicate his life to making natural healing accessible, effective, and empowering.

What makes the method different.

Four things that set the Amari Method apart from every other approach Garrett trained in over 25 years. Each one is a deliberate inversion of how most care is delivered.

01

Yoga blocks, foam rollers, gymnastic rings. Garrett sees what you can't and guides you through it.

03
