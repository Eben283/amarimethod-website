Transforming Lives Through Natural Healing

The story of Dr. Garrett Hewstan and the creation of the Amari Method

A Child in Pain Became a Doctor Determined to Help Others

Every journey to wellness begins with a story—and Dr. Garrett Hewstan's began with a child in pain and a mother who refused to give up.

The Turning Point

As a child, Dr. Hewstan spent countless nights in hospital oxygen tents, struggling to breathe through severe asthma. Medication offered temporary relief, but it left him dependent and afraid. Everything changed when his mother took him to a chiropractor whose gentle care freed him from medication dependency and gave him the freedom to thrive.

That experience planted a seed: Natural healing is possible. A different approach can change everything.

The Promise That Changed Everything

Years later, during his final year of chiropractic school, something happened that would shape Dr. Hewstan's life's mission. A student clinic patient named Phyllis shared heartbreaking news: her 11-year-old grandson, Amari, had just passed away from complications of asthma.

Amari was the same age Dr. Hewstan had been when chiropractic care saved his life. Garrett couldn't help but wonder: What if I could have helped him?

In that moment, Dr. Garrett made a promise—to Amari, to his younger self, and to every person struggling with chronic pain or dysfunction—that he would dedicate his life to making natural healing accessible, effective, and empowering.

Every person who finds freedom through the Amari Method carries forward the spirit of that promise—and honors the boy whose name reminds us why this work matters.

25 Years of Healing Practice

Since that promise, Dr. Hewstan has spent over 25 years refining his healing methodology. He's worked with thousands of patients—from athletes to office workers, from acute injuries to decades of chronic pain. Through careful observation and continuous learning, he developed what is now the Amari Method: a seven-step protocol that delivers immediate relief while teaching clients how to care for their bodies for life.

No ongoing dependency. No quick fixes. Just sustainable healing that starts in the first session and continues for a lifetime.
