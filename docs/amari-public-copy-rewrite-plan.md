# Amari Public Website Rewrite Plan

**Status:** In progress · August 8, 2026

## Objective

## Scope boundary

This rewrite applies to non-blog public pages only. **Do not change blog or journal posts** as part of this work; their editorial claims and search strategy need a separate review.

Make the public site describe Amari as a remarkable physical product: private, hands-on guided movement that can change how the body moves and feels. The paid $29 Amari Assessment is the clear first step.

## Message system

1. **Subtle input. Unmistakable result.**
2. **Private, hands-on guided movement that changes how your body moves and feels.**
3. **For pain and restriction that get in the way of work, training, and life.**
4. **Start with a $29 Amari Assessment.**

The longer practice is an evolving teacher-led physical practice. It is not a fixed protocol course or a promise that a person never needs skilled guidance again. Living Practice is visual recall for work learned in sessions.

## Rewrite sequence

### 1. Homepage and Assessment — implement first

- Replace the self-directed “balance” hero with the central contrast and literal description.
- Make the $29 / 50-minute in-person Assessment visible in the first CTA, first-visit section, and closing CTA.
- Explain that the Assessment is a private working session, not a free consultation or an obligation to purchase a longer practice.
- Remove “dependent,” “teach yourself,” and protocol-library framing from the primary story.

### 2. Primary explanation pages

- **How It Works:** explain active, hands-on, precise whole-body guidance; replace the old 8-protocol curriculum and “body heals itself” story.
- **In-Person Sessions:** retain practical preparation and hands-on specificity; replace balance/protocol copy.
- **About:** make Garrett’s role that of a skilled teacher helping a client learn what they cannot initially find alone; keep emotional-release language out.
- **Living Practice:** reposition as between-session visual cues, not a standalone replacement for in-person work.

### 3. Condition pages and editorial library

- Retain specific pain-entry SEO paths, but remove unsupported diagnostic, root-cause, comparative, and cure claims.
- Do not publish a condition outcome as product proof until the case has verified facts, consent, and appropriate review.
- Rebuild page by page around accurate examples and genuine consented proof.

### 4. Supporting surfaces

- Update metadata, social copy, FAQ/schema, navigation, booking language, and CTAs to match the new product description.
- Preserve all functioning booking/payment/calendar behavior; copy work must not alter those paths.

## Acceptance criteria

- A new visitor can identify what Amari is, whether it is relevant, and how to begin in the first viewport.
- The $29 Assessment is clear without discount language.
- No primary page frames Amari as generic mobility, a course, PT homework, “balance,” “healing yourself,” or passive treatment.
- No public copy makes unsubstantiated numerical, diagnostic, comparative-medical, tissue-repair, cure, or guaranteed-result claims.
