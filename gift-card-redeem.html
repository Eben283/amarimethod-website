Someone Gave You a Gift

A session that could change the way your body feels. Here's how to use it.

  1. Find your gift card code

    Check the email or message from the person who sent it. Your code looks like a short string of letters and numbers.

  2. Book your session

    Go to our booking page and choose your session type. You can book an initial session, follow-up, or apply it toward a series.

  3. Apply your code at checkout

    On the payment screen, enter your gift card code in the coupon or gift card field. The amount will be applied automatically.
