Booking rules

Use this page to distinguish session duration, the post-session buffer, and the spacing of bookable starts. GHL remains the availability source of truth.

The three separate rules

DurationHow long the practice member is in session.
BufferProtected time after the session.
IntervalSpacing between bookable start times.
BlockDuration + post-session buffer.
Do not equate interval with duration. A 50-minute Assessment has a 20-minute turnover buffer; the hourly start rhythm is a separate scheduling rule.
