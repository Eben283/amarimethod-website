How the Amari Method Evolved from 25 Years of Healing Practice

8 min read • February 2026 • By Dr. Garrett Hewstan

My Journey to Creating the Amari Method

My journey into solving this problem began with my own experience healing from severe childhood asthma—something conventional medicine said was impossible. That experience taught me that the body has incredible healing capacity when you address the root cause rather than manage symptoms.

Years later, I heard about a young boy named Amari who lost his life to similar complications. That's when I committed to finding a better way—a method that would empower people to heal themselves rather than depend on the healthcare system.

After 25 years as a bodyworker, I found myself wanting to help people in a bigger way. I wanted to empower my clients with these tools because they're the ones I use consistently that bring me incredible change and relief on a daily basis. They're the culmination of 25+ years as a yoga teacher, massage therapist, chiropractor, and having attended countless hours of continuing education. Some I've intuitively developed myself. Whenever I'm experiencing tightness, pain, or discomfort in my body, these tools remedy it every time.

I wanted my clients to not need me (or other practitioners) as much, and I want to be able to empower people around the world as well. I want to help people no longer be dependent on the healthcare system. I want to provide them with an opportunity to change the root causes of their problems rather than simply going for pain relief or temporary fixes.

The Birth of the Amari Method

This decades-long exploration has led me to develop the Amari Method, a set of seven essential steps that anyone can perform anywhere to maintain their body's natural balance and proper function.

What makes this protocol different is that it isn't theoretical. Every step has been tested, refined, and proven effective through my own practice and with thousands of clients over more than two decades. These aren't complicated techniques requiring special equipment or professional supervision. They're simple, accessible, and profoundly effective.

Understanding Pain Differently

There's a consistent pattern in the culture of lower and upper back pain. This is mainly because we sit too much and only move in habitual, less-than-optimal ways. The vast majority of people I encounter struggle with one of these chronic pain patterns, which I simply call defense physiology.

These issues aren't random; they're clear signals that our bodies are out of alignment and trying to get our deeper attention. In this culture, we often see pain as adversarial, wanting to get rid of it at all cost. But pain is not the problem—it's the messenger. We need to address the root cause and see pain as instructional. It's your body's way of telling you that it's not happy with what you're doing.

This shift in perspective is crucial. Once you understand pain as information rather than the enemy, everything changes. You stop seeking quick fixes and start seeking real solutions.

A Solution That Works

The Amari Method directly addresses these root causes. People get immediate relief from years and decades of chronic pain. Why? Because we're not just treating the symptom—we're fixing the underlying dysfunction that created the pain in the first place.

These seven core exercises represent the best of my trial and error. This protocol is profound, simple, and incredibly effective. You will gain an entirely different relationship to your body as you use these steps ongoingly. You will see your body changing right before your eyes. That's why I consider them the center of my practice.

The beauty of the Amari Method is that it works whether or not you understand the biomechanics behind it. You don't need advanced knowledge. You just need to follow the steps consistently.

Universal Application

Whether you're dealing with carpal tunnel syndrome, elbow issues, shoulder pain, spinal problems, hip discomfort, knee trouble, or foot pain, the Amari Method provides a solution. And it feels good while you're doing each step. They're easy to do.

The Amari Method refines itself over time as you become more in tune with your body and what it's really needing. You have it for the rest of your life. It can free you from constant dependence on professional intervention.

This isn't about replacing healthcare practitioners—it's about empowering you so you don't need constant care. It's about giving you the tools to maintain your own health and prevent problems before they start.

The Philosophy Behind the Method

What I've learned over 25 years is that our bodies want to function optimally. They're not designed to be in pain. Pain is a symptom of dysfunction, and dysfunction comes from misalignment, poor posture, weak stabilizer muscles, and habitual movement patterns that reinforce these problems.

By addressing these root causes—by restoring alignment, correcting posture, strengthening the right muscles, and creating new movement patterns—we remove the conditions that create pain. The body heals naturally when we stop doing the things that hurt it.

This is the principle behind everything I teach. It's not complicated. It's actually beautifully simple once you understand it.

My Mission

My mission is simple: I want people to understand their bodies and have the ability to heal themselves. By sharing the Amari Method, I'm putting the power of healing back into people's hands, giving them the tools they need to maintain their body's natural balance for life.

The Amari Method does not just treat pain, but rather it empowers people with the knowledge they need to live life on their own terms. No more dependence on medications, injections, or endless appointments. Just a set of tools that work, that you can use anywhere, anytime.

That's the vision I've worked 25 years to create.

FAQ: The Amari Method Story

What is the Amari Method?

The Amari Method is a 7 core exercises developed after 25 years of healing practice. It's a set of techniques anyone can perform anywhere to maintain their body's natural balance and proper alignment, addressing the root causes of chronic pain.

How long did it take to develop the Amari Method?

The Amari Method evolved over 25+ years of experience as a yoga teacher, massage therapist, chiropractor, and bodyworker, plus countless hours of continuing education and intuitive development. Each step has been tested and refined through practical experience.

Can anyone use the Amari Method?

Yes. The 7 core exercises are designed to be universal—whether you're dealing with carpal tunnel, shoulder pain, back pain, hip discomfort, knee trouble, or foot pain. Anyone can perform these steps anywhere, regardless of age or fitness level.

Is pain always bad?

Pain is not the problem—it's a messenger. It's your body's way of telling you something isn't right with your alignment or movement patterns. By understanding pain as instructional rather than adversarial, you can address the root cause instead of just managing symptoms.

What does 'defense physiology' mean?

Defense physiology refers to the chronic pain patterns that develop when our bodies are misaligned and moving in habitual, less-than-optimal ways. These patterns are our body's way of protecting itself from further dysfunction, but they can become problematic if they persist.

Will I need to see a practitioner forever?

No. The Amari Method empowers you to heal yourself. It frees you from constant dependence on professional intervention by giving you the tools to maintain your body's natural balance for life. Professional sessions can help you learn, but the goal is independence.

How quickly does the Amari Method work?

Many people experience immediate relief from years or decades of chronic pain when they begin the Amari Method. The results continue to deepen as you become more in tune with your body's needs and consistent with the practice.

What's the goal of the Amari Method?

The goal is to empower people to understand and heal their own bodies. It's about changing the root causes of pain rather than seeking temporary fixes, and ultimately helping people live life on their own terms without dependence on the healthcare system or constant professional care.
