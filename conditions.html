Lower back

Lower back pain

The most common pattern — and the most over-treated. Usually a compression and support problem from years of sitting, not a disc you have to live with.

→ Neck

Neck pain

The head drifts forward and the neck carries a load it was never built for. Relieve that load and most chronic neck pain and tension resolves.

→ Shoulder

Shoulder pain

Impingement, rotator cuff issues, and frozen shoulder are almost always a shoulder-blade and upper-back problem — not the joint itself.

→ Hip

Hip pain

Hip pain and tightness usually trace back to how the pelvis sits and how the surrounding muscles share the load. Rebalance them and the joint frees up.

→ Knee

Knee pain

The knee is where problems above and below it show up. Correct the hip and ankle mechanics and the abnormal load comes off the knee.

→ Sciatica

Sciatica

Shooting leg pain is a compressed sciatic nerve — disc, piriformis, or general compression. Find where, then decompress the pathway at the source.

→ Jaw

TMJ & jaw pain

Clenching, clicking, and jaw pain are usually driven by forward head posture and neck tension — not a damaged joint. Fix the posture, unload the jaw.

→ Foot

Plantar fasciitis

Heel and arch pain that's worst on the first steps of the day. The foot is paying for what the hip and ankle above it stopped doing.

→ Whole body

Chronic pain

Pain that has moved around, lingered for years, or never had a clear cause. The Amari Method looks at how force moves through the whole body.
