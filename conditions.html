The approach

Different pain. The same way back.

Every one of these shows up in a different place, but the principle underneath is the same: something is overworking because something else stopped doing its job. Garrett finds the pattern and teaches you to rebalance it yourself.

Lower back

Lower back pain

The most common pattern, and the most over-treated. Usually a compression and support problem from years of sitting, not a disc you have to live with.

Neck

Neck pain

The head drifts forward and the neck carries a load it was never built for. Relieve that load and most chronic neck pain resolves.

Shoulder

Shoulder pain

Impingement, rotator cuff pain, and frozen shoulder are almost always a shoulder blade and upper back problem, not the joint itself.

Hip

Hip pain

Hip pain and tightness usually trace back to how the pelvis sits and how the surrounding muscles share the load. Rebalance them and the joint frees up.

Knee

Knee pain

The knee is where problems above and below it show up. Correct the hip and ankle mechanics and the abnormal load comes off the knee.

Jaw

Jaw & TMJ tension

Clenching, clicking, and jaw pain are usually driven by forward head posture and neck tension, not a damaged joint.

Sciatica

Sciatica

Shooting leg pain from a compressed sciatic nerve. Find where the compression starts, then decompress the pathway at the source.

Foot

Plantar fasciitis

Heel and arch pain that's worst on the first steps of the day. The foot is paying for what the hip and ankle above it stopped doing.

How the method addresses it

Whatever brought you in, the process is the same.

01

Assessment

Garrett assesses how your body moves, whatever condition brought you in. Where it's overworking, where it's shut down. He's looking at your whole body, beyond the part that hurts.

02

Guided protocols

Using simple props (yoga blocks, foam rollers, gymnastic rings), Garrett guides you through protocols matched to your specific pattern, not a generic routine for your diagnosis.

03

What changes

Most clients feel a noticeable shift during the first session. The overworked areas release. The underworked areas start to re-engage. You feel the difference before you leave.

04

Take-home practice

You leave with a short practice for what was worked on that session, about five minutes on your living room floor. It keeps your body moving in the right direction between sessions.
