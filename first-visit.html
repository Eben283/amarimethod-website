The session

Your first session, minute by minute.

Sixty minutes total, at our San Francisco office or over video. Same structure either way.

01 Before you arrive

Wear something you can move in

Stretchy pants or shorts, a t-shirt or athletic top. Pants without belt loops if you can help it. Nothing else to prepare. Garrett provides every prop you'll need.

02 About 20 minutes

The assessment

Garrett watches how you stand, move, and hold yourself. He's not looking at the part that hurts. He's looking for the pattern behind it, what's overworking, what's gone quiet. Two simple tools, a suspension squat and a bridge on a yoga block, usually reveal it in minutes.

03 About 30 minutes

Your first protocol

Using a yoga block, a foam roller, or the rings, Garrett guides you through the protocol your body is asking for. You're moving, not lying still while someone works on you. He adjusts it in real time as your body responds.

04 Last 10 minutes

What you leave with

Most people notice a shift before the session ends: taller, lighter, less braced. You also leave with a short practice for what you worked on, about five minutes, done on your living room floor, to hold the change until your next session.

What people ask before they book

The questions before the first session.

I'm not in good shape. Can I still do this?
Yes. You're not being tested on fitness. Garrett is watching your movement pattern, not your conditioning. Stiff, out of shape, mid-injury, all of that is normal on day one.
What if nothing happens?
Most people feel something in the first session. If you don't, Garrett keeps working with you at no additional charge until you do. That's the guarantee, not a hope.
Do I need to be flexible?
No. Flexibility isn't the goal, balance is. Some of the fastest changes happen in bodies that are tight or stiff, because what's out of balance is obvious.
What should I bring?
Just yourself, dressed to move. Garrett provides every prop: yoga blocks, foam rollers, rings. Nothing to buy before you come in.
